## The method, and its error

**What is read.** `ioreg -rn AppleSmartBattery` shows a `PowerTelemetryData` dictionary with no privileges asked for. `SystemLoad` is the power the machine is drawing, in milliwatts. `AccumulatedSystemLoad` is the sum of one such sample a second, with a count of the samples. The registry refreshes these about once a minute.

**What the probe established** (`power-probe.json`, 200 seconds): the telemetry is there; three refreshes, 60 seconds apart; voltage times current agrees with the reported power in to within 0.1%; the two energy accumulators agree to within 4 to 8% (the sum of the load samples is the larger). The plan's note said units and accuracy were to be established. Units are established by that agreement. **Accuracy against an external meter is not established**: nothing here was compared with one.

**What follows from a refresh once a minute.** Energy cannot be taken per request. A model's 72 units run as one block; the block's energy is the accumulator's change over it, with the minute at each end split by time. The energy of those two minutes bounds the error of the split, and it is not small: 12% of the 7B model's block, 31% of the 1.5B's, 12% of llama's. A task's energy is its share of its block's by time.

**What "system" means.** The whole machine: display, the editor that was open, the runtime, everything. On external power, battery full. Idle power before the campaign measured 2.0 W over five refreshes, with the display asleep; the column net of idle differs from the gross one by under 4% and decides nothing.

## H-10: inconclusive

*Energy per solved task ranks models differently from tokens per second.*

Three models, 24 tasks, three repetitions, one request a task: 216 runs in 1,866 seconds (estimated 50 minutes from the datasheets; "about 60 minutes" in the manifest's notes), with nothing else of this project running.

| Model | Block | Energy | Bound on the ends | Solved | Energy an attempt | Energy a thousand tokens | Tokens a second |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| qwen2.5-coder:1.5b | 381 s | 25,372 J | 7,969 J | 0 of 72 | 352 J | 456 J | 160.3 |
| qwen2.5-coder:7b | 506 s | 36,603 J | 4,569 J | 0 of 72 | 508 J | 1,725 J | 49.5 |
| llama3.2:3b | 975 s | 62,669 J | 7,548 J | 0 of 72 | 870 J | 764 J | 91.7 |

No model solved a task, so energy per solved task has no value for any of them and the two rankings cannot be compared. Amendment 2 named this case in advance: inconclusive.

Energy per attempt decides nothing by registration. It is worth one sentence because it does not follow speed: the 3B model generates almost twice as fast as the 7B and spent 1.7 times the energy an attempt, because it wrote four times as many tokens. The bounds on the block ends are too wide to rest more than that on these numbers.

These are also the module's only throughput numbers taken on a quiet machine: 49.5, 160.3 and 91.7 tokens a second, against 15 to 23 for the 7B model earlier the same day with other applications holding memory.

## Scope notes

- **Energy per accepted line**, which the plan lists, is not reported: nothing was accepted, because nothing was solved.
- **One laptop.** On a desktop Mac the dictionary is absent; the probe then exits 3 and says the telemetry is unavailable, and the outcome there would be "unavailable on this hardware".
- **The idle baseline** was to be three minutes; the log shows the wait took 348 seconds, and the display was asleep for it. It is a floor for this machine, not the power of the machine as it is used.
- **The load average rose during the campaign** (2.1 at the start, 5.4 at the end): that is the runtime itself.
