## H-8: inconclusive, and no badge ships

*Sample agreement or token probability predicts hunk correctness better than chance by a pre-registered margin.*

Both pinned models, 60 tasks, five seeds each at temperature 0.2, with a log-probability for every token: 600 runs in 4,081 seconds (estimated 2.6 hours from the datasheets; "about 2.5 hours" in the manifest's notes).

| | |
|---|---|
| Units (a model and a task) | 120 |
| Units whose first reply placed no edit, so that there was no hunk to label | 58 |
| Labelled hunks | 120 (the registration asked for at least 500) |
| Correct hunks | 6 (5 from the 7B model, 1 from the 1.5B) |

| Estimator | Hunks | AUROC | ECE | By the registered thresholds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| agreement of five samples | 120 | 0.507 [0.384, 0.687] | 0.215 | inconclusive |
| least token probability of the block | 114 | 0.599 [0.299, 0.885] | 0.093 | inconclusive |

Amendment 2 fixed, before the campaign, that an estimate is not decided with fewer than 20 hunks of either class. There are 6 correct hunks. So nothing is decided, and the intervals above say the same thing in another way: both include 0.5.

What can be said without deciding anything: agreement is mostly at its floor. For 103 of the 120 hunks, the first sample's hunk appeared in no other of the five samples. At temperature 0.2 these models seldom write the same edit twice.

**The badge.** None ships. The product names no estimator (`ONYX_HUNK_CONFIDENCE`), `describeHunkConfidence` returns nothing whatever the estimate, and the review pane renders no confidence. `docs/SUPPORT.md` says so.

## Scope notes

- **No estimate is computed in the product.** Agreement needs five samples of every edit and token probability needs the runtime's log-probabilities on every request. Neither is plumbed into the editor, because no estimator was supported. What exists is the rule that would gate a badge and the study that did not earn one.
- **The grounding flag** is not an estimator here: in a single-turn task the whole file is in the prompt, so it is constant. It is H-5's subject.
- **Six hunks had no token probability**: their block could not be placed in the reply's text. They are counted and left out of that estimator.
- **Results size.** `traces/records.jsonl` is 13 MB: every token of 600 replies with its log-probability.
