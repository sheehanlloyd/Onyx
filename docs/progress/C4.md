## The transcripts are constructed, not recorded

The plan's done-when asks for transcripts from one Ollama thinking model and one LM Studio reasoning model,
recorded and replayed as fixtures. No reasoning model is installed on this machine, and this session may not
download one. The four transcripts were therefore written by hand in the stream shapes the two runtimes
document: thinking in `<think>` tags in the content; thinking in `reasoning` (Ollama's OpenAI-compatible
endpoint); thinking in `reasoning_content` (LM Studio); and tagged thinking cut by the output cap. The file
says so at the top.

What that means:

- R-26 passes, on constructed inputs. It shows that Onyx does the right thing with replies of these shapes.
  It does not show that a particular model produces these shapes.
- `docs/SUPPORT.md` lists reasoning models as **handled, not validated against a model**, and lists none as
  supported. That is the plan's instruction for models that were not tested.
- To close this properly: pull one thinking model under Ollama and one under LM Studio, record one tool-call
  turn from each with the mock's request logging, replace the constructed bodies with the recorded ones, and
  rerun R-26. The tests take the bodies as data; nothing else changes.

## Decisions made inside the item

- **One filter in front of everything.** The loop filters the stream once, before the text stream that
  recognises tool calls written as prose, so the repair path, the constrained envelope and the transcript
  all see the answer only. The one-shot parsers (edits, findings) call `stripReasoning` themselves because
  their callers collect a whole reply.
- **An unfinished thought is cut off, not answered.** Thinking opened and never closed ends the run as
  failed even when the stream ended with a finish reason, because `finish_reason: length` in the middle of
  thinking is exactly that. Nothing inside the open thought is parsed.
- **The note carries at most 4,000 characters** of thinking, start and end. It is on the timeline and in
  the journal, where the diagnostics export treats it as content (length only by default).
- **Thinking is not sent back.** Later turns carry the model's answer and its tool calls, not its
  reasoning. That is how the runtimes' own chat templates treat it.
- **`<thinking>` is accepted as well as `<think>`.** Nothing else is: a tag vocabulary that grows by guess
  would start eating real text.

## Limits, stated

- **A lone `</think>`** (some templates emit only the closing tag) is left in the text. Handling it would
  mean treating everything before it as thinking, on a guess.
- **Thinking counts against the output cap.** B13 caps a reply at 15% of the context, at most 4,096 tokens.
  A reasoning model that thinks at length can use it up; the reply then ends mid-thought and is reported.
  Raising the cap for reasoning models needs a measured profile field, which L5 can provide.
- **Autocomplete does not use reasoning models specially.** Fill-in-the-middle requests are unchanged.
