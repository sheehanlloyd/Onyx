## Decisions made inside the item

- **A quote may begin in the middle of a line.** That is an exact match, and the end-to-end mock and small
  models both quote a line without its indentation. One case is refused: a block of several lines (on either
  side) that begins inside a line's indentation. Its first line would keep the file's indentation and the
  others the model's, which is a re-indent by omission and can move a Python statement out of its block.
- **Overlapping occurrences count.** `aa` is in `aaa` twice.
- **Line endings.** The pure applier compares bytes: a CRLF quote against LF text, or the reverse, is
  refused and the refusal says the line endings differ. In the product the change set works on the buffer's
  lines joined by `\n` and applies through the text model, so a CRLF file is edited with its endings kept and
  never rewritten; B8's integration tests exercise that.
- **Inline edit, tournament and the repository benchmark** call the same applier and are exact now too.
- **One call still fails whole** when any of its blocks is refused, as before.

## Found while doing this

- **The I-08 check had a false positive that the baseline table was hiding.** SEARCH text copied from a
  numbered `readFile` result ("12| text") is decoded by the product (B1, R-24); the check compared the text
  as sent. With the table entry gone the campaign reported 14 such episodes in 2,000. The check now takes
  the known wrapper off the way the product does, and holds the decoded text to the same rule.
- **The published parser chart** counts the drifted quote as recovered. It is redrawn under C8, which gives
  charts a manifest.
- **Rebasing is stricter than it will stay.** A hunk is carried over with one line of context and has to be
  unique in the new buffer; in a file with repeated lines more hunks are dropped (with the existing
  notification) than B7's three lines of context will drop.
