## H-6: supported, on the second campaign

*At temperature 0 with a fixed seed on one machine, at least 90% of multi-turn trajectories repeat exactly.*

Both pinned models, the first 20 of H-2's tasks, five repetitions each, seed 1, temperature 0, the product's configuration. 200 agent runs a campaign; every one has more than one turn.

| Campaign | Identical to the unit's first | Units with all five identical | Divergences: runtime | Divergences: harness | Outcome changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| first (`2026-11-22-determinism`) | 146 of 160 (91.3%; 85.8% to 94.7%) | 35 of 40 | 0 | 14, all at turn 1 | 0 |
| second (`2026-11-22-determinism-2`), with the fix | 160 of 160 (100%; 97.7% to 100%) | 40 of 40 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

**The first campaign is invalid by the registration's own rule**, and the reason is a defect it found. All 14 of its divergences are in the first request of a run, before the runtime has said anything. The prompt's background lists the files most recently committed; the runtime service kept that list for a minute; after a campaign checked out its next task the prompts went on naming the previous task's files, and when the minute ran out between two repetitions, the two requests differed. `2026-11-22-determinism/NOTES.md` has the evidence. Amendment 4 was committed and pushed before the second campaign ran.

**The second campaign decides H-6: supported.** The identical share is 100% with a lower bound of 97.7%, against thresholds of 90% and 80%.

What the result does and does not cover:

- **The runtime did not diverge once in 320 comparisons** across the two campaigns. Ollama 0.33.0 does not say whether it honoured a seed, so every run is in the stratum "not known to be honoured", as Amendment 3 said it would be.
- **One machine, one runtime, one session.** No second machine was available. Repetitions ran back to back with the model resident; nothing here says a trajectory repeats after a reload, on another machine, or under memory pressure of another kind.
- **These are short trajectories.** Under the product's configuration these models read a file and answer (L13): a run has two or three turns. A long trajectory has more places to diverge.
- **The replay through L8** that the registration asks for could not be made: the rig holds its own fixture files, not a worktree of this repository. Amendment 2 put the request-digest comparison in its place before any campaign ran, and that comparison is what found the defect.

## What the defect means for the other campaigns

Every agent-loop campaign before the fix ran with it: L9's engine run, H-2's campaign (L13), L12's injection run and the first anchors campaign. For up to a minute after each checkout, a prompt's background named up to five files of the previous task. Amendment 4 says what is run again (H-6 and H-4, where the stale background fell on one arm) and what is not (H-2, whose outcome is a floor of zero in every configuration, and the injection run, whose result is that no planted effect happened). `docs/progress/L13.md` and `L12.md` were written before the defect was known; this section is the correction to both.

In the product the same list is still kept for a minute. Outside a campaign nothing checks out revisions behind the editor's back, and a list one minute old is what it was designed to be.
