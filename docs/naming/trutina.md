# Trutina

Preliminary research, not legal clearance.

Date of all checks: 2026-11-22. Nothing was registered, reserved or purchased. Availability can change at any time.

**Verdict: DISQUALIFIED.** An AI model is published under this name, an AI company has reserved the PyPI name, and a model evaluation harness uses it. Each is small, but together they occupy the AI-measurement meaning this product would want.

## The name

Latin for a balance or pair of scales. Length: 7 letters. As a command and bundle name: `trutina`.

## Domains
