## A harness that adapts to the model

Pick **Auto** in the model picker and Onyx routes every request:

- quick edits go to fast, small models
- implementation and debugging go to your strongest tool-callers
- routing blends measured speed, tool-call reliability, and how often you kept each model's answers

A 7B model gets a compact prompt and fewer tools; a 70B model gets the full harness. Everything is learned locally from your own usage.
