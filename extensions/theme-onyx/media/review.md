## Commit and review, locally

- The **sparkle** button in the Source Control input turns your staged diff
  into a commit message with a local model. It writes into the box — nothing
  is committed for you.
- **Onyx: Review My Changes** puts your working tree past an adversarial
  reviewer prompt. Findings land on the control-plane timeline as clickable
  `file:line` links.
- In the editor, **Fix with Onyx** appears on any diagnostic and **Explain
  with Onyx** on any selection.

The reviewer reports; it never edits. Every one of these runs on the same
local models as chat, and shows up in the same timeline.
